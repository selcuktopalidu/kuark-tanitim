## How a KuARK game gets harder (English)

File: how-difficulty-works-en.mp4 (5:03, subtitles: how-difficulty-works-en.en.srt). Playlist: For Teachers. Audience: not made for kids.

**Title (EN)**

```text
How a KuARK game gets harder: twenty levels, five stages | KuARK · İDÜ Lab
```

**Description (EN) · YouTube**

```text
Every game has twenty levels in five stages, and every level runs the same loop: predict, try, see, explain, transfer. Using the levels of the projectile game, the film explains the difficulties placed on purpose, their learning-science terms, seeded scenarios and server-side checking. For teachers, faculty and education researchers.

KuARK · İDÜ Lab is a free science-games platform developed at Izmir Demokrasi University: 150+ games from primary school to university, in 25 languages.
Platform: oyun.idu.edu.tr (opening soon)
About the project: https://selcuktopalidu.github.io/kuark-tanitim/
```
