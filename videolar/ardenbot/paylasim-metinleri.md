## ArdenBot: The class helper on the board (English)


**Description (EN) · YouTube**

```text
In board mode the whole class plays one experiment together. ArdenBot sits in the panel under the board: he introduces the experiment, calls the turns and the points, and runs the vote and the questions. The film covers team and timer settings, the class prediction, the discussion timer, the class success card, the privacy rules and how to open board mode. For teachers and lecturers.

ArdenBot’s picture was made from a photo of Arden, used with his parents’ permission.
```
