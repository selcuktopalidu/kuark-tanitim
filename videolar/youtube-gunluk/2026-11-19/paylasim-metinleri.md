**Description (EN) · YouTube**

```text
A tour of the Azerbaijan–Türkiye corner of Science Bridges: Baku-born Lotfi Zadeh introduced fuzzy sets in 1965; in fuzzy logic, membership is a degree between 0 and 1. The Shamakhi Astrophysical Observatory, founded in 1959, bears the name of the 13th-century astronomer Nasir al-Din al-Tusi. In the 2025 call, TÜBİTAK and the Azerbaijan Science Foundation jointly funded seven research projects. The corner has four cards, a five-statement “True or false?”, a three-situation “What would you do?”, a 40-minute lesson for teachers, three games and its sources. Science note: the Tusi couple.
```
