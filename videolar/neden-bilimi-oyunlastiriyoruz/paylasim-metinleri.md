**Description (EN) · YouTube**

```text
What if science class felt like your favourite game? For many students science means reading, memorising and fearing the wrong answer, and too many decide early that science is not for them. That is a design problem, so we redesigned it: you start with a guess, try it, see what happens and explain why. Every board runs on real equations, from the lever to the projectile to al-Biruni’s mountain, and every level follows the same five steps: predict, try, see, explain, transfer. The answer appears only after you solve the level.

KuARK · İDÜ Lab is a free science-games platform developed at Izmir Demokrasi University: 150+ games from primary school to university, in 25 languages.
Platform: oyun.idu.edu.tr (opening soon)
```
