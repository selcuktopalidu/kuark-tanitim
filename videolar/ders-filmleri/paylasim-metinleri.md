## High-school class (English)

File: lesson-high-school-en.mp4 (1:50, subtitles: lesson-high-school-en.en.srt). Playlist: For Teachers. Audience: not made for kids.

**Title (EN)**

```text
A lesson in a high-school class: Projectile Launch and two right angles | KuARK · İDÜ Lab
```

**Description (EN) · YouTube**

```text
A board-mode lesson in a high-school class: Projectile Launch. The launch speed is 25 m/s and the ball must land 55.4 m away. The red team tries 45°; the ball flies 63.7 m, too far. The class votes: a smaller angle means a shorter range. The blue team hits the target with 30°; the second solution is 60°, and the two add up to 90°. The film is a dramatisation; Projectile Launch was designed by Prof. Dr. Selçuk Topal.
```
